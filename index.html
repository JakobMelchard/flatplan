flatplan: shortcuts & how-to

Touch & Pencil

tapSelect; in a drawing tool, place a point
dragMove the selection, else pan
pinchZoom and pan with two fingers
PencilOnce used, only the Pencil places points; fingers pan and zoom
handleDrag the dot above an item to rotate (15° steps)
two-finger tapUndo; the pill that appears offers Redo (also the toolbar arrows)
multi-select buttonNext to Select: taps add / remove furniture, walls, doors; drag draws a box
long-pressSame without the button: on something toggles it, on empty space starts a box
selected wallDrag it to move; walls joined to it stretch along

Walls

tapPlace corner; tap first corner to close, last corner to finish
length + arrowExact segment in cm (bottom bar)
0-9 EnterSame from the keyboard, along the cursor axis
ShiftLock to 90°
CtrlDisable grid snap

Furniture

tap / drag cardPlace in the middle / where you drop it
dragStops at walls and door swings, snaps to wall faces
R / Shift+RRotate ±90°
Q / ERotate ±15°
↑↓←→Nudge 1 cm (Shift = 10)
Ctrl+D / DelDuplicate / remove
Shift+click / dragAdd to selection / box select (Pencil: drag on empty space)
Ctrl+ASelect all items
Ctrl+Z / Shift+Ctrl+ZUndo / redo

Doors & windows

D / NTool; then tap a wall
dragSlide along the wall
bottom barHinge side, swing side
Door swing zone is solid for furniture
